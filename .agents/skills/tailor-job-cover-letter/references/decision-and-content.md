# Cover-Letter Decision And Content Contract

## Verified Evidence Pool

Use only evidence already present in the approved resume package or durable
candidate answers. Strong recurring examples include:

- M.S. Robotics and Autonomous Systems, AI concentration, Arizona State
  University, expected July 2026
- B.S. Computer Science, Arizona State University, May 2025
- Efficient TransFuser camera-LiDAR perception and localization work:
  168M to 50M parameters, 70% reduction, 8.7% held-out loss improvement,
  and 25.4% BEV segmentation improvement
- Parrot MiniDrone physical-hardware work involving localization, sensor
  fusion, PID control, logging, tests, and safety checks
- 3D motion planning with Python RRT, ROS, and Gazebo
- Spider CAD Robot
- Factory Automation System for Semiconductor Process Simulation, phrased
  honestly as coursework or work in progress where applicable
- DigiClips, Odoo, and TicketDex software-engineering internships and their
  verified metrics
- Expert Linux proficiency

Use the role-specific approved resume as the final authority because its
wording may qualify adjacent experience more precisely.

## Recommended Structure

1. Date
2. `Hiring Team`
3. Company
4. `Re: <exact role>`
5. `Dear Hiring Team,`
6. Opening fit statement
7. One or two evidence paragraphs
8. Company-specific contribution paragraph
9. Professional close
10. `Sincerely,`
11. `George Jobi Perangattu`

## Quality Rules

- Make the first 75 words specific to the role.
- Use concrete evidence, not adjective clusters.
- Mention two or three projects or experiences at most.
- Do not duplicate the professional summary verbatim.
- Do not keyword-stuff.
- Do not discuss salary or EEO information.
- Mention OPT or future sponsorship only when the employer explicitly requests
  that information in the letter and the user has approved including it.
- Avoid unsupported claims such as `expert in PLC programming`, `production
  ROS engineer`, or `years of professional robotics experience`.

## Form Responses

When the employer asks for a short answer:

- obey the stated word or character limit;
- answer the exact prompt directly;
- retain the same evidence and truthfulness rules;
- omit letter formatting, addresses, date, and sign-off unless requested.
- store the exact approved text in `application-responses.json`;
- do not repeat the same response in an optional PDF;
- when both outputs are justified, give the PDF a different purpose and use
  different evidence rather than expanding the text response.
